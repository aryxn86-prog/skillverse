Quick answers

Is the job data real?

No — the 15 roles and all company postings are synthetic sample data for this academic prototype.

Are my applications sent to companies?

No. The apply flow is a demonstration and everything stays in your own browser.

How is the match % calculated?

Using cosine similarity between your selected skills and each role's weighted requirements. See the About page for the formula.

Can you add a new role?

Yes! Choose "Suggest a role" in the form and tell us which one.
