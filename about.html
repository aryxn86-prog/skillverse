The problem

Career advice is often too generic to act on.

Students, career switchers and self-taught learners struggle to see which roles fit their current skills, what's missing for a role they want, and what to learn next. Skill Atlas answers all three — and shows its working.

Which jobs fit my skills? What am I missing? What should I learn next? The three questions Skill Atlas is designed to answer

Aim

To develop and evaluate a transparent prototype that recommends suitable tech roles from a user's existing skills, identifies the important missing skills, and suggests related skills to learn next.

How scoring works

Each role is a vector of skill weights (0–1). Your selection is a binary vector. The match is their cosine similarity:

match = Σ ws / ( ‖job‖ × √n )

Gaps are the role's skills you don't have, sorted by weight. Adjacent skills come from co-occurrence across all roles.
